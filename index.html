The platform

Eight tools. One platform.

Every module works independently — and together. Like eight arms working in parallel, so nothing falls through the gaps.

01
Overview

Your event at a glance. Guest confirmations, budget status, checklist progress — live.

02
Checklist

Pre-built templates for every event type. Add deadlines, assign owners, track progress.

03
Budget

Track every expense and income line. See what's paid, what's outstanding, and your net balance in real time.

04
Guests

Full guest CRM. Import lists, manage RSVPs, track dietary needs, assign tables and rooms.

05
Rooms

Add your accommodation inventory. Assign guests to rooms and track booking and payment status.

06
Run Sheet

Build a minute-by-minute timeline for the day. Check items off in real time, on any device.

07
Table Plan

Drag guests to tables, track seating assignments, export or print for your team.
